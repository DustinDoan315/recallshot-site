# RecallShot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

RecallShot is made by Doan Khanh Dong. It helps you search and organize screenshots on your iPhone. It works without an account.

## What stays on your device

These stay on your device. We do not receive them and have no server that stores them:

- The screenshots you choose to add
- Text that RecallShot reads from them, using on-device recognition
- Your notes, tags and collections

Your original images stay in Apple Photos. RecallShot's local index is stored on your device and is set to be excluded from iCloud backup.

## Purchases

RecallShot uses RevenueCat to process and restore RecallShot Pro purchases made through Apple. For this, RevenueCat and Apple process purchase information, such as the products you bought and when, along with a randomly generated identifier that RecallShot does not link to your name or email address. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Screenshots and their text are not sent to RevenueCat.

## What we do not do

RecallShot has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not sell your data.

## Choices you control

- **Photos access:** change or turn off access in iOS Settings at any time.
- **Export:** you can create an export file and share it. It contains text from your screenshots, which may be sensitive. Where it goes after that is up to you.
- **Clean Up:** RecallShot only deletes an original from Photos if you select it in Clean Up and approve the iOS confirmation.
- **Erase:** Settings > Erase RecallShot data removes saved references, text, notes, tags and collections from the device. Deleting the app also removes its local data.

## Children

RecallShot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
