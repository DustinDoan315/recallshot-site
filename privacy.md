# Glimpsed Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Glimpsed is made by Doan Khanh Dong. It helps you search and organize screenshots on your iPhone or Android phone. It works without an account.

## What stays on your device

These stay on your device. We do not receive them and have no server that stores them:

- The screenshots you choose to add
- Text that Glimpsed reads from them, using on-device recognition
- Your notes, tags and collections

Your original images stay in your phone's photo library: Apple Photos on iPhone, and your Screenshots folder on Android. Glimpsed's local index is stored on your device. On iPhone it is set to be excluded from iCloud backup. On Android, Glimpsed is excluded from Android backup, so its data is not copied to Google's cloud.

## Purchases

Glimpsed uses RevenueCat to process and restore Glimpsed Pro purchases made through Apple on iPhone or Google Play on Android, and to give us purchase reports such as subscription charts. For this, RevenueCat and the store you bought from process purchase information, such as the products you bought and when, along with a randomly generated identifier that Glimpsed does not link to your name or email address. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/), [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Screenshots and their text are not sent to RevenueCat, Apple or Google.

## What we do not do

Glimpsed has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not sell your data.

## Choices you control

- **Photos access:** change or turn off access at any time, in iOS Settings on iPhone or in Android Settings > Apps > Glimpsed > Permissions on Android.
- **Export:** you can create an export file and share it. It contains text from your screenshots, which may be sensitive. Where it goes after that is up to you.
- **Clean Up:** Glimpsed only deletes an original from your photo library if you select it in Clean Up and approve your phone's system confirmation.
- **Erase:** Settings > Erase Glimpsed data removes saved references, text, notes, tags and collections from the device. Deleting the app also removes its local data.
- **Purchase records:** to ask us to delete the purchase record RevenueCat keeps for your anonymous identifier, email us at the address below. Your purchase history with Apple or Google stays with them and is managed in your Apple Account or Google Account.

## Children

Glimpsed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
