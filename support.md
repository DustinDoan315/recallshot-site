# RecallShot Support

Contact: doankhanhdong3105@gmail.com. Please don't send screenshots that contain private information.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?** On your iPhone. RecallShot has no account and does not upload your screenshots or their text.

**How do I restore Pro?** Open Settings > Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. This restores Pro access only. Screenshots and text stay on the device where you saved them.

**How do I cancel my subscription?** Open Settings > Manage Subscription in RecallShot, or manage it in your Apple Account's subscriptions.

**Why can't RecallShot see some screenshots?** You may have given limited Photos access. Go to Settings > Photos Access in RecallShot to change it.

**Does removing a screenshot from RecallShot delete the original?** No. Originals are only deleted if you select them in Clean Up and approve the iOS confirmation. They then go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

**How do I delete my data?** Use Settings > Erase RecallShot data, or delete the app.
