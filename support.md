# Glimpsed Support

Contact: doankhanhdong3105@gmail.com. Please don't send screenshots that contain private information.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?** On your phone. Glimpsed has no account and does not upload your screenshots or their text.

**How do I restore Pro?** Open Settings > Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google Account (Android) you bought Pro with. This restores Pro access only. Screenshots and text stay on the device where you saved them. A purchase made on iPhone does not unlock Pro on Android, and the other way round.

**How do I cancel my subscription?** Open Settings > Manage Subscription in Glimpsed. You can also manage it in your Apple Account's subscriptions on iPhone, or in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions on Android.

**Why can't Glimpsed see some screenshots?** You may have given limited photo access. Go to Settings > Photos Access in Glimpsed to change it.

**Does removing a screenshot from Glimpsed delete the original?** No. Originals are only deleted if you select them in Clean Up and approve your phone's system confirmation. On iPhone they then go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

**How do I delete my data?** Use Settings > Erase Glimpsed data, or delete the app. To ask us to delete your purchase record at RevenueCat, email the address above.
